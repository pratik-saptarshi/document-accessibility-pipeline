# Backlog Refinement Panel — 2026-10-13

**Status: Proposed recommendations; no tracker or roadmap decisions adopted.** This report records a six-role planning review and judge synthesis. It does not change Beads, the traceability catalog or generated matrix, the canonical roadmap, product scope, or issue status. Implementation remains subject to the relevant user decisions and evidence gates.

## Executive synthesis

The repository has a credible planning foundation: existing-PDF remediation is the accepted primary lane, generation is separate, AFP is its own feasibility track, the four document families are named, and automated validation is not treated as a substitute for human semantic and assistive-technology review. The exported blocker graph is structurally intact, but the panel found measured contractual traceability and definition-of-done gaps: 12 of 20 catalog task mappings conflict with explicit Beads acceptance-case lists; eight implementation successors have planning-only completion criteria, with one analogous AFP assessment gap; and 9 of 28 closed issues are specification-only. These are observed gaps, not work pending confirmation. Case ownership and the exact reconciliation remain decisions for the responsible owners.

The panel recommends a short, decision-led sequence: scope the next remediation increment; finish a finite, evidence-based remediation feasibility gate; move only a narrow, real structure-changing remediation slice into a candidate/review flow if that gate passes; keep generation feasibility parallel and its production lane separate; and defer broad family expansion and release hardening until evidence justifies them. PR #38's checkbox appearance-state work is useful bounded spike evidence, not writer selection or product approval.

**First-use-case question for the product owner:** “For the first useful remediation increment, which matters most: simple born-digital letters, statements or invoices with tables, or interactive forms?” Remediation remains the accepted primary lane. Family, language, feature exclusions, and other scope details remain unapproved. The answer should scope the first useful increment; it need not select a library.

No production workflow, product engine, writer, first family, release protocol, or user outcome has been demonstrated. Accordingly, statements such as “reduce review time” remain hypotheses until a baseline and measured outcome are agreed.

## Connection to the original goal

The [initial repository description](https://github.com/pratik-saptarshi/document-accessibility-pipeline/blob/341368185ebbe67883b0c619f8c1f31fd9cd3383/README.md) was “Enterprise document generation and remediation accessibility pipeline.” The [first roadmap](https://github.com/pratik-saptarshi/document-accessibility-pipeline/blob/d65e7112501fa5f55679bc409a925271684a3815/docs/roadmap/product-roadmap.md) framed planning as non-approved schedule/budget work and organized the backlog under `dap-jo9`. Current product scope makes existing-PDF remediation the accepted primary lane and new accessible-document generation a separate lane across statements, invoices, correspondence, and reports; AFP remains a separate feasibility track. The accepted [PDF/UA-2 target and Go-first/permissive-dependency constraints](https://github.com/pratik-saptarshi/document-accessibility-pipeline/blob/5d1de2a09a0b2782f6e185c1622ff0d0fb3fd118/docs/decisions/0001-pdf-ua-2-validation-target.md) are useful boundaries, while writer, generation engine, workflow, profiles, reviewer authority, and release eligibility remain unresolved.

The goal can be expressed as four user outcomes to validate: produce a useful candidate; preserve content and meaning; make unsupported, uncertain, and review-required cases visible; and reduce an agreed, measured risk or effort. Current evidence supports planning and synthetic feasibility only. It does not yet show a user can complete the remediation journey, a production transformation is safe, review effort falls, or a conformance claim is justified. Confidence is **high** in the measured structural/contract gaps, **medium** that correspondence is the best first use case, and low/unproven for writer or engine feasibility. Product owns scope and policy; engineering resolves capability and license evidence; authorized reviewers provide semantic and assistive-technology acceptance.
