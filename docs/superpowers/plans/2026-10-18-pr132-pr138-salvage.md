### Benchmark study gates

- Seed each warm measurement state with an untimed successful bootstrap before
  recording any warm observation.
- Label checkout and no-checkout scopes truthfully. No-checkout timing must
  include the supported per-run pinned source archive extraction and build, or
  be named an orchestrator microbenchmark.
- Retain a wrong-version control whose binary digest matches the manifest so
  the version check, rather than checksum mismatch, rejects it. Retain checksum
  failure and exit-code controls; use synthetic payloads only.
- Generate the raw observations from the committed harness and identify the
  exact harness bytes/revision that actually ran. Report the resolved Go,
  operating-system, CPU, and `GOMAXPROCS` settings.
- Define `delta = median(bootstrap+verify) - median(bootstrap-only)`;
  `relative increase = delta / median(bootstrap-only)`; and, separately,
  `verify share of combined time = delta / median(bootstrap+verify)`. Keep the
  denominators explicit and preserve negative/noisy deltas as observed.
- If retaining the study's registered trigger, calculate it as an absolute
  positive delta of at least 2 seconds **or** a positive relative increase of
  at least 25% over the bootstrap-only baseline. Report “threshold met” as a
  measurement result only. It does not recommend removing verification or
  override the safety value of the check.
- Preserve historical evidence with a clear superseded-result notice when a
  corrected run replaces it. Do not silently overwrite or relabel old numbers.
- Do not close `dap-5hy.3` without its exact acceptance evidence and an allowed
  Beads workflow.
