### Benchmark study gates

- Seed each warm measurement state with an untimed successful bootstrap before
  recording any warm observation.
- Require and report all four timing states independently: checkout cold,
  checkout warm, no-checkout cold, and no-checkout warm. Preserve every raw
  sample and the repetition count for each state; do not infer or substitute a
  missing cell from another state.
- Before each measured cold arm, create an independent fresh tools root and
  cache directory, even when the two modes are paired in one repetition. Do not
  let either arm reuse state changed by the other. Treat the scoped `GOCACHE`,
  `GOMODCACHE`, installed-tool tree, and (for no-checkout) generated build
  output as cold state; reset them between arms. Record the reset procedure
  with the raw evidence. Warm cells instead use a separately seeded state
  prepared by one untimed successful bootstrap.
- Label checkout and no-checkout scopes truthfully. No-checkout timing must
  include the supported per-run pinned source archive extraction and build, or
  be named an orchestrator microbenchmark.
- Retain wrong-version controls whose binary digests match the manifest so
  version checks, rather than checksum mismatches, reject them. Cover every
  tool in a multi-entry synthetic manifest with checksum and version validation
  controls, or add a direct test proving the common validation path applies to
  every manifest entry. Retain checksum-failure and exit-code controls; use
  synthetic payloads only.
- Generate the raw observations from the committed harness and identify the
  exact harness bytes/revision that actually ran. Report the resolved Go,
  operating-system, CPU, and `GOMAXPROCS` settings.
- Preserve the original #132 declaration from 2026-10-17 as a historical
  criterion: **mean standalone `--verify` cost >= 2 seconds OR verify cost >=
  25% of combined total setup time**. Do not relabel it as the later paired-
  median rule; use historical raw data for it only if those measurements are
  available and support the calculation.
- For the corrected 2026-10-18 paired-order study, define the prespecified
  marginal-median estimator as `delta = median(bootstrap+verify) -
  median(bootstrap-only)`; `relative increase = delta /
  median(bootstrap-only)`; and separately `verify share of combined time =
  delta / median(bootstrap+verify)`. Keep both denominators explicit and
  preserve negative/noisy deltas as observed. Name this a difference of
  marginal medians, not a median of within-repetition differences.
- The marginal-median rule (`positive delta >= 2 seconds OR positive relative
  increase >= 25% of the bootstrap-only baseline`) was recorded in the
  acceptance plan at 2026-10-18T12:50Z, before the corrected run at
  13:10:51Z. Call it a **revised exploratory trigger** for that corrected run,
  not the original #132 registered trigger. Also report the median of
  within-repetition verify-minus-bootstrap differences as a separate
  paired-delta sensitivity analysis; do not silently substitute it for the
  recorded estimator. Do not apply either result retroactively to the
  2026-10-17 measurements or unrecoverable intermediate runs. A 25%
  combined-time share and a 25% baseline-relative increase are not equivalent:
  the former equals a 33⅓% baseline-relative increase. Report “threshold met”
  as a measurement result only; it does not recommend removing verification or
  override the safety value of the check.
- Preserve historical evidence with a clear superseded-result notice when a
  corrected run replaces it. Do not silently overwrite or relabel old numbers.
- Do not close `dap-5hy.3` without its exact acceptance evidence and an allowed
  Beads workflow.
